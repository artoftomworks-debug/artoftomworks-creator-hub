SCENE CONTINUATION – CREATOR HUB 0.15.0

1. Show a completed generated video. Choose Next scene segment immediately
   below it, or use the button on a sequence card.
2. The Hub reads the last decoded frame and the selected video's actual saved
   model, LoRAs, strengths and sampling graph. Your current draft stays untouched.
3. Describe the next event, select a camera and optionally specify audio,
   duration and seed. Audio direction becomes an Audio: paragraph in the positive
   prompt; sound generation depends on the selected model/workflow capabilities.
4. Load plan into creation view, review it, then explicitly start generation.
   Loading or closing the preparation panel never submits inference.
5. Finished continuations appear in the horizontal sequence. Open the preview
   for a larger view and choose a take on each card.
6. New take of this segment reuses its original starting frame and settings,
   restores the event/camera and defaults to seed +1. Existing takes are kept.
   The first segment reuses its original input image; later segments use their
   predecessor's last frame. Retrieving the original input may need the engine.
7. Assemble in video studio sends the coherent selected sequence to the real
   editing timeline, including source audio. Trim, adjust sound and explicitly
   export there. Opening the timeline does not render anything.

Up to 24 segments per sequence. Changing an earlier take does not attach a
later segment generated from a different predecessor. Those branches remain
available when their original take is selected again.
Keep both generated source files and the Hub data folder. Selection is stored
in data/scene-sequences.json; relationships and recipes live in data/runs.

H3 and supported simple LTX graphs are supported. Standard text-only sources
receive a start-image branch while preserving model and sampling settings.
Complex multi-stage or postprocessing-only sources may lack a reusable recipe;
use the original generated video take in that case. The transferred frame
anchors the starting appearance; describe the desired continuing motion in the
new prompt. Each segment is generated separately, including any model audio.
